# Joke review — weakest 30%, with rewrites

Scope: every authored line the porch can say (about 150 bits, jokes, greetings, replies,
lures and farewells; ~500 lines across Patch, Strong Bad, the Sorting Hat, the doorbell and
Voldemort inside). Live AI replies are not included — they're written fresh each visit.

What the strong bits have in common (keep doing this): a specific, concrete image ("stepped
on a Lego", "security gourd", "beefy frog", "I'm screwed to the wall"), a twist on the last
word, and the doorbell as a deadpan straight man. What the weak ones share: they explain
instead of land, end on a flat instruction, or reference something that's gone (the goblin).

Ranked worst first. **Now** → **Proposed**.
